## What is Cloud Computing:
Delivery of computing services over the internet.

## Benefits of Cloud Computing:
easy, fast and adaptable deployment.
High scalability both horizontally and vertically.
On demand provisioning, cost control.
Reliability and predictability.

## Shared Responsability:
Depending on the clould service model, the responsability of maintaining the service is shared between the cloud service provided and the customer.
- The service provider is **always** responsible for maintaining the physical side like the datacenter and the physical network.
- The customer is **always** responsible for maintainung and securing the data uploaded to the cloud, the connected devices and the people accounts and identities.
- Depending on the service model, responsabilites can be shared or assigned to either side, like the operating system, network controls and applications.

## Cloud models:
-Private cloud.
-Public cloud.
-Hybrid Cloud.
-Multicloud.

## Consumption based model
You only pay for the ressources you use. Cost effective compared to investing in your own infrastructure.
No risk of over or under spending on ressources.
